Resume & Cover Letter Templates

Design Pairs

The resume and cover letter are presented as a design set for branding and visual alignment with potential employers.

PDF Output

Resumes are set to print at a ?continuous single page for ease of submission and modern reading on computers, not physical printouts.

Light/Dark Modes

There are light and dark toggles for publishing your resume to a web host or other service.
